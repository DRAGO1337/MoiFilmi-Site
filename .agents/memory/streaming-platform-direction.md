---
name: Streaming platform direction
description: The user's intended product and requested production stack for the streaming site.
---

The user wants a website where people can upload movies and series for everyone to watch. They requested Next.js and Supabase, and a free video host such as Cloudflare Stream. The initial homepage was built in the selected React/Vite artifact; that does not settle the production stack.

**Why:** The requested platform direction is not discoverable from the starter code, and the first deliverable intentionally covered only the homepage.

**How to apply:** Before adding the production backend, confirm whether to continue with the current React/Vite frontend or migrate to Next.js. Check current hosting plans and limits before describing a video provider as free.
